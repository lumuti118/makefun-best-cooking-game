# 🍔 Burger Station

**Grill it. Stack it. Serve it fast!**

Burger Station is a bright, fast diner game about one satisfying physical
verb: building a tall burger, layer by layer, against the clock. Tickets
come in on the order board, each with its own patience timer. You grill the
patty on a three-zone grill — low, medium, or fierce high heat — flip it at
the right moment for a perfect sear, then stack bun, patty, cheese, lettuce
and tomato in exactly the ticket's order and serve before the customer walks
out. Leave a patty too long and it burns to charcoal; get greedy on high
heat and the grease flares. Three burgers to master: the Classic, the Cheese
Deluxe, and the towering Double Trouble.

## How to play

- **1–5** pick an ingredient (bun, patty, cheese, lettuce, tomato) — or click a bin on the counter
- **Space** grills a patty / flips it / places the selected ingredient on the stack
- **Enter** serves the burger — match the ticket exactly for the full price plus a patience bonus
- **C** clears the plate
- Click a grill zone to grill there directly, or click the big green SERVE button

## Features

- 3 burger recipes with different stacks, prices and patience bonuses
- 3-zone grill (LOW / MED / HIGH) with doneness bars, a green perfect window, burn risk and grease flares
- Order board with 3 live tickets and patience countdowns
- 3-minute shifts, score, served count, perfect-serve bonuses and a best score
- Synthesized WebAudio sizzle, flips, chimes and buzzes — no audio files, no network
- Keyboard and mouse controls, deterministic `?shot=start|play|end&seed=1` screenshot scenes

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
